📐 Video Mapping & Crops

In Tri-Pumpkin Split, your wide 16:9 video is divided into 3 segments. Each pumpkin slot can be independently positioned, scaled, and keystoned onto its real pumpkin!

Horizontal & Vertical Crop Regions

1. Left Pumpkin Crop H: 0%–33% | V: 0%–100%

💡 Pro Projection Mapping Tip

Press M (Mapping Wireframe) to see the 4-corner perspective quads projected onto your physical pumpkins. Drag the corners in the center column until the video faces map perfectly onto your pumpkins!
